Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This page explains what Origin Client ("Origin", "we") collects when you use the Origin launcher, the Origin mod in game, and this website, why, who it goes to, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. It is written from what our code actually does. Questions go to will@willhenry.me.

1. The Short Version

  • Your Microsoft and Minecraft sign-in stays between you, Microsoft and Mojang. It is stored encrypted on your PC and never reaches Origin's servers.
  • Your worlds, settings, mods and logs stay on your PC.
  • If you are signed in, Origin's online service knows your Minecraft name and UUID, whether you're online, which version you're playing and on which server, your friends, and your messages.
  • If you buy something, Stripe handles the payment. We keep which Minecraft account owns which cosmetic.
  • The website has no analytics, no ad trackers and no cookies of its own.

2. Signing In With Microsoft

To play, you add a Microsoft account in the launcher. You sign in on Microsoft's own sign-in page. The launcher then talks to Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft's services to prove you own Minecraft and to get your Minecraft name, UUID and skin. The launcher only asks Microsoft for permission to sign in to Xbox Live and to stay signed in.

The launcher saves the sign-in key Microsoft gives it, plus your last Minecraft session, in accounts.json inside %LocalAppData%\OriginLauncher. Both are encrypted with Windows' own protection for your Windows user, so the file is useless if it is copied to another PC. None of these keys are ever sent to Origin.

3. What Stays on Your PC

Everything below lives in %LocalAppData%\OriginLauncher and is never uploaded by Origin:

  • Your accounts (encrypted, see above) and launcher settings.
  • Your game folders: worlds, servers list, options, mods, resource packs and shader settings.
  • Settings Sync. It copies your keybinds, video settings, HUD layout, mod settings and server list between the Minecraft versions on your PC. It does not upload them anywhere.
  • Logs, crash reports and caches (skins, the launcher's news feed).
  • The Hypixel API key you paste into Origin's Hypixel mod, if you use it. It is only ever sent to Hypixel (see section 7).

4. Friends, Parties and Chat

When you're signed in, the launcher and the game connect to Origin's online service, which runs on Cloudflare. To prove who you are without handing us any sign-in key, your launcher or game asks Minecraft's services for a certificate they issue to every player, signs a short message with it, and sends us your UUID, your name and that signature. We check it against Mojang's published keys.

What the service stores:

  • Your Minecraft name and UUID, so friends can find you by name.
  • A sign-in session. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of its key, and it expires after 24 hours.
  • Your status: whether the launcher or the game is open, whether you're in the menus, in singleplayer or on a server, the server's address (never a private home-network address), your Minecraft version, and when you were last seen.
  • Your "Appear offline" setting. When it is on, friends see you as offline.
  • Your friends list, friend requests and the players you've blocked.
  • Direct messages and party chat you send.
  • Party details: who is in your party, invites, pings and party warps. Your position, dimension and health are shared with your party only inside a world hosted by a party member's own game over a home network. On every other server they are never sent.

Your friends see your status, the server you're on and your version. Other Origin players can see a small Origin badge next to your name while you've been in game on Origin in the last 10 minutes. The "playing Origin" count on the launcher's Home screen is just a number; it does not list anyone.

5. Cosmetics, the Store and Origin+

On the Store you type the Minecraft name the cosmetics are for. The page looks that name up with PlayerDB (playerdb.co) to find the UUID and skin, and shows heads and skins from Crafthead and mc-heads.net. Your bag and the name you typed are saved in your browser's own storage, so they survive a refresh. They stay in your browser.

At checkout, the page sends the UUID, the name and the items to Origin's service, which opens a Stripe Checkout page with those three things attached. You pay on Stripe's page; your card details go to Stripe, not to us. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what it keeps. Once Stripe tells us the payment went through, we store:

  • For each cosmetic: the Minecraft UUID, the item, the Stripe payment ID and the time. A full refund or a dispute removes it.
  • For Origin+: the Stripe subscription ID, the Minecraft UUID, the name typed at checkout, the Stripe customer ID, the membership status and when the paid period ends. You can cancel, change your card or get receipts on Stripe's billing page.
  • For a free store code: the code, the UUID and the name it was used for, and when.
  • What you're wearing and any height tweaks, set from the game.

What a player owns and wears, and whether they have Origin+, is public: every Origin player's game asks for it to draw cosmetics on the players around them.

6. Discord

While the game runs, Origin tells the Discord app on your PC (if it's open) what to show as your status: "Minecraft" plus the version, one of "In the menus", "Singleplayer" or "Multiplayer", and how long you've been playing. It never includes the server address. This goes only to the Discord app on your PC; Discord then shows it according to your Discord settings and Discord's own privacy policy.

The launcher's home banner also asks Discord's public invite page for our server's name, icon and member count. That request carries nothing about you.

7. Other Services the Launcher and Game Use

  • Mojang: game files, versions and Java runtimes, and players' heads and skins for your friends list.
  • Fabric: the mod loader for each version.
  • Modrinth: the mods Origin installs, and the shader and resource pack browsers in game. When "Fix Compatibility" looks for a missing mod, only that mod's ID is sent.
  • Forge, CurseForge's file server and OptiFine (with backup mirrors on BMCLAPI and the Internet Archive): only for 1.8.9.
  • GitHub: the website's download button, and the launcher's update check, which asks GitHub for our newest release. A copy of the launcher's news feed is also read from GitHub if originclient.org can't be reached.
  • originclient.org: the launcher reads its news feed from this site. That request sends no cookies and no account details.
  • Hypixel: only if you paste your own Hypixel API key into Origin's Hypixel mod. The game then sends that key and the UUIDs of players it looks up to Hypixel, and nowhere else.

The launcher's home banner has an ad mode for an ad network. It is switched off. If it is ever turned on, the ad page runs in its own separate browser storage in the launcher, apart from your Microsoft sign-in, and the launcher adds nothing about you to it. We will name the ad network on this page before it goes live.

8. This Website

originclient.org is hosted by Vercel. The site loads no analytics, no ad trackers and no outside scripts, and our code sets no cookies. The only thing it saves in your browser is the Store's bag and typed name (section 5). The Store also talks to the services listed in section 5. The download button downloads the installer from GitHub.

9. Crash Reports

When the game crashes, the launcher reads the crash report on your PC to name the mod behind it. Nothing is uploaded. "Copy report" puts a cleaned-up copy on your clipboard, with sign-in keys, email addresses, server addresses and your Windows user name removed, so you decide where to paste it.

10. How Long We Keep Things

  • Sign-in sessions: 24 hours.
  • Direct messages and party chat: 30 days, then deleted automatically. When the last member leaves a party, that party's chat is deleted right away.
  • Pings: 1 minute. Party warps: 2 minutes. Party invites: 10 minutes. Records used to stop spam: 1 hour. You leave a party automatically after an hour with Origin closed.
  • Friends, requests and blocks: until you or the other player remove them.
  • Your name and UUID, your status and last-seen time, your "Appear offline" setting, cosmetics you own and wear, Origin+ records and used store codes: kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Everything on your PC: until you delete it.

11. Deleting Your Data

  • On your PC: uninstall Origin and tick "Also delete my worlds, settings and accounts". That deletes the whole %LocalAppData%\OriginLauncher folder, including your saved sign-ins.
  • Friends: remove or block anyone yourself in the Friends list.
  • Everything else on Origin's service: there is no delete button yet. Email will@willhenry.me with your Minecraft name and we'll delete it. Deleting your purchases also removes those cosmetics from your account.
  • Payments: Stripe keeps its own payment records under its own privacy policy.

12. Changes

When Origin starts collecting something new, or stops, this page changes and the date at the top moves.
