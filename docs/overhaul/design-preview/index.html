8 · Numerals on art

One rule: the number stands on the ground line

Every version number set on art, on Home and on every card, follows the same rule. It stands in the picture’s bottom-left corner, inside a shade that grows from that corner, with its first figure’s ink on the gutter and its baseline on the ground line. Its figures are a quarter of the picture’s height, so it scales with the art, not with the type around it. Neutral white, proportional lining figures, never tinted.

Card. Gutter 12, ground line 12 above the bottom edge, figures 25 % of the card’s height, eyebrow 8 above the figures, range 12 after them on the baseline.

Home. Gutter 24, ground line 24 above the control strip, figures 25 % of the window’s height, eyebrow 15 above them. The same corner shade.
