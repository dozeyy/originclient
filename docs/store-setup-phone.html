Add your address for sales tax

The one thing Stripe Tax is still missing

Open Tax settings ↗
  1. Tap Get started (or Edit next to Head office).
  2. Enter your business address. If you don't have a business, that's your home address.
  3. Leave everything else as it is. Prices and the tax type are already set by the store.

Until this is done, checkout shows "Checkout isn't available right now". Stripe watches your sales and tells you under Registrations when a state or country says you have to start collecting tax there.

Buy something, then refund it

A real $5.99 charge you get straight back

Open the store ↗
  1. Add a $5.99 item (like the Origin Cape), type dozeyy as the name, and pay.
  2. Check your email for the receipt.
  3. Open Origin on that account. The item should be in Cosmetics within a minute.
  4. Refund it in Stripe: Payments → the payment → Refund → full amount.
  5. Back in game, the item should be gone within a minute.
