Try Origin+, then cancel it

A real $4.99 charge you get straight back

Open the store ↗
  1. On the store, type dozeyy as the name, pick Monthly in the Origin+ card and join.
  2. Back on the store with the same name, it should say you're a member and show the 20% lower prices.
  3. Tap Manage or cancel, sign in with the email you paid with, and cancel. It stays active until the month ends.
  4. Refund the $4.99 in Stripe: Payments → the payment → Refund. Then end it now: Customers → that customer → the subscription → Cancel → Immediately. The store should stop showing you as a member within a minute.
  5. One thing to check with whoever does your taxes: Origin+ is filed under Stripe's tax category "Video Games, streamed, subscription". It's the closest match Stripe has. Change it on the Origin+ product if they say otherwise.
