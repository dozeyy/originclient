Origin title-screen icons - third-party art and licences
==========================================================

textures/gui/title_icons.png (built by make_icons.py) contains pixels from these three packs,
used as drawn. The only changes are a recolour to white (252) with alpha/luminance snapped at
50 %, a 1 px dark (42) drop shadow at (+1,+1), and a 1:1 crop to each shape.
