Origin title-screen icons - art and licences
==============================================

textures/gui/title_icons.png is built by tools/title-icons/origin_icons.py (2026-11-14).

Cosmetics - a t-shirt
Realms    - a castle keep with a pennant
  Origin's own icons. The designs were generated with Recraft and then set by hand, pixel by
  pixel, on the sheet's 1:1 grid in Minecraft's icon tones (white 252, greys 214/170/110, a 1 px
  dark 42 drop shadow at (+1,+1)). No third-party pixels.

Quit - X
  Kenney 1-Bit Pack (version 1.2) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  Source:  https://kenney.nl/assets/1-bit-pack (kenney_1-bit-pack.zip),
           Tilesheet/colored-transparent_packed.png, tile row 13, col 40
  Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
           Credit is not required; given here anyway.
  Changes: recoloured to white (252), a 1 px dark (42) drop shadow at (+1,+1), cropped.

Vanilla Minecraft sprites (Friends = toast/social_interactions, Language, Accessibility) are NOT
in the sheet: the mod draws them at runtime from the game's own resources by ResourceLocation,
so nothing of Mojang's is redistributed.
